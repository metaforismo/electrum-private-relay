# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project. Be constructive,
assume good intent while evaluating evidence critically, and focus discussion on
the technical work and its impact.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate
disclosure of private information, sustained disruption, and publishing security
details that put users at immediate risk.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this standard
and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Reports should be
sent privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile until a dedicated
conduct address is documented.

Maintainers will protect reporter privacy as far as reasonably possible and will
avoid conflicts of interest when handling a report.
